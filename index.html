A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Still have something on your mind?

Who is this mentorship for?

Dentists who earned their degree outside the U.S. and are preparing to apply to advanced standing DDS/DMD programs. Both first-time applicants and reapplicants are welcome.

When should I start preparing?

Ideally, before an interview or bench test invitation arrives. Your consultation helps you work backward from your application timeline and identify where to focus first.

Are sessions online or in person?

Both. Interview coaching is available over video. Bench coaching can include reviewing photos and videos of your preparations, and in-person typodont sessions can be arranged.

How many sessions will I need, and what does it cost?

Your plan and fees depend on your starting point, goals, and test dates. After a free consultation, you’ll receive a recommended plan and a quote.

Do you help with the INBDE or TOEFL?

Our main focus is bench preparation, interview coaching, and application strategy. Exam timelines and study planning can be discussed as part of mentorship.

Does coaching guarantee admission?

No. Admission decisions are made by each school. Our role is to help you prepare thoughtfully, improve your skills, and present your experience clearly.
